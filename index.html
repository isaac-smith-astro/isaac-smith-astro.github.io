About Me

I am a senior undergraduate student studying astrophysics and physics at the University of Michigan. My research experience spans observational astronomy, computational astrophysics, and material science. I am particularly interested in computational high-energy astrophysics. Currently, I am working with Prof. Oleg Gnedin to analyze model predictions for the globular cluster populations of massive galaxies. Outside of research, I enjoy running, hiking, cooking, and presenting planetarium shows for the Student Astronomical Society at Michigan!

Research & Projects

Globular Cluster Systems of Giant Galaxies

Jan 2026 – Present | Department of Astronomy, University of Michigan

Generated predictions from a hierarchical model of globular cluster (GC) formation and disruption for the evolution of GC systems of brightest cluster galaxies. Trained machine learning classifiers to distinguish ex-situ and in-situ GC based on their observable properties and demonstrated that ML methods only performed marginally better than a simple metallicity split.

Multiscale Modeling of Dynamic Behavior of Fluid Filled Pores

Summer 2026 | Computational Physics Workshop, Los Alamos National Laboratory

Ran fully-resolved hydrodynamic simulations a single helium bubble in a copper cube to evaluate applying the homogenized Johnson Spall damage model to metals with helium bubble impurities. Identified limitations of the Johnson Spall model in this context and recommended potential improvements.

Strong Gravitational Lensing in Galaxy Clusters

Dec 2024 - Feb 2026 | Department of Astronomy, University of Michigan

Developed strong gravitational lensing models for galaxy clusters using new James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) imaging from the Strong LensIng and Cluster Evolution (SLICE) survey. Contributed to data reduction of archival Hubble Space Telescope imaging for SLICE collaboration.

Electron Temperatures of Planetary Nebulae

May 2025 | ASTRO 426, Kitt Peak National Observaory / University of Michigan

Conducted end-to-end observational research project investigating the electron temperatures of planetary nebulae. Wrote a proposal for telescope time, collected spectroscopy at MDM Observatory, analyzed and presented our results.
