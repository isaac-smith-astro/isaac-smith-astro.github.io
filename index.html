About Me

I am a senior undergraduate student studying astrophysics and physics at the University of Michigan. My research experience spans observational astronomy, computational astrophysics, and material science. My primary research interests include numerical relativity and computational high-energy astrophysics. Currently, I am working with Prof. Oleg Gnedin on applying the globular cluster evolution model in Chen & Gnedin (2024a) to globular clusters in galaxy clusters. Outside of research, I enjoy running, hiking, cooking, and presenting planetarium shows at the Michigan Student Astronomical Society Open Houses!

Research & Projects

Globular Clusters in Galaxy Clusters

Jan 2026 – Present | Department of Astronomy, University of Michigan

Analyzed predictions from the semi-analytical model in Chen & Gnedin (2024a) for the evolution of globular clusters in galaxy clusters. Trained machine learning classifiers to distinguish ex-situ and in-situ globular clusters based on their observable properties.

Multiscale Modeling of Dynamic Behavior of Fluid Filled Pores

Summer 2026 | Computational Physic Workshop, Los Alamos National Laboratory

Ran fully-resolved hydrodynamic simulations a single helium bubble in a copper cube to evaluate the homogenized Johnson Spall damage model for pore growth and spallation. Pin-pointed limitations of the Johnson Spall model and recommended avenues for improving the model.

Strong Gravitational Lensing in Galaxy Clusters

Dec 2024 - Feb 2026 | Department of Astronomy, University of Michigan

Developed strong gravitational lensing models for galaxy clusters using new James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) imaging from the Strong LensIng and Cluster Evolution (SLICE) survey. Contributed to data reduction of archival Hubble Space Telescope imaging for SLICE collaboration.

Electron Temperatures of Planetary Nebulae

May 2025| ASTRO 426, Kitt Peak National Observaory / University of Michigan

Conducted end-to-end observational research project investigating the electron temperatures of planetary nebulae. Wrote a proposal for telescope time, collected spectroscopy at MDM Observatory, analyzed and presented our results.
